At a Glance

Dr. Mohammad Ali Zinnah has been teaching at the Institute of Education and Research (IER), University of
Dhaka for almost thirty years. At present, he is the senior professor at this institute next to the director. He has
been awarded a PhD from the School of Educational Studies, University Sains Malaysia, Penang, Malaysia in
2010. His area of specialization is curriculum studies and social science education. Dr. Zinnah is highly
acquainted with the overall education system of Bangladesh, its present situation, challenges, and prospects.
He has vast experience in guiding academic research at Master of Education, M.Phil., and PhD levels, as well
as conducting fundamental research in the field of education as a consultant on behalf of diﬀerent government
institutions, NGOs, INGOs, and international organizations like the Ministry of Education, Save the Children,
JICA, the British Council, and UNESCO. Dr. Zinnah has also worked as a core skills trainer in the Connecting
Classrooms Project of the British Council from 2016 to 2020. He has a good number of publications in national
and international journals on diﬀerent issues of primary, secondary, and non-formal education, as well as
teaching and teacher education. He was a member of the national curriculum committee for social studies at
the primary level in 2004. Following this curriculum, he wrote the Social Studies Textbook for grade V under
joint authorship. He was a member of the OBE curriculum framework development committee formed by the
University Grants Commission (UGC), Bangladesh. He has presented a significant number of papers in diﬀerent
national and international conferences. He worked as a convener for developing the OBE curriculum for the
Pre-professional and Professional Master of Education Program at the Institute of Education and Research
(IER), University of Dhaka. He has been also working as the Convener of OBE Curriculum Development
Committee for undergraduate and post-graduate programs at IER, DU. Professor Zinnah has contributed to the
development of OBE curriculum as the key expert in different departments like Department of Library Science
and Information Management, Department of Zoology, Department of Music, Department of Persian Language
and Literature, and Institute of Modern Languages in the University of Dhaka as well as various departments of
Islamic University, Bangladesh. He coordinated a cross-cultural Project funded by the IDRC, Canada and GPE
KIX which has been implemented in five country contexts from May 2021 to November 2023. He also worked
