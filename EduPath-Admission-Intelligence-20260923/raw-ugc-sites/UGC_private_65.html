A Few Words About the University

Britannia University (BU) (Bengali: ব্রিটানিয়া বিশ্ববিদ্যালয়) is the UGC Approved First Private University in Cumilla. The university is located at Paduar Bazar, Bishwa Road, Cumilla, Bangladesh.
