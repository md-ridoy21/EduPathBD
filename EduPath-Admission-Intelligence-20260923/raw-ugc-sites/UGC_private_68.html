Vice Chancellor's Message

Chittagong Independent University (CIU) began its journey as a branch campus of Independent University Bangladesh (IUB) and transitioned into a full-fledged university in compliance with the Private University Act of 2010 under the Education, Science, Technology and Cultural Development Trust (ESTCDT). ESTCDT was established in 1992 as a philanthropic body with a strong commitment to take Bangladesh's higher education to global standards. Under ESTCDT, Independent University, Bangladesh (IUB) was founded in 1993 in Dhaka and the Chittagong Independent University (CIU) in 2013.
