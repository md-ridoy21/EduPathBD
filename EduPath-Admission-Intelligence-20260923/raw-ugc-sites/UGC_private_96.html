About KKBAU

Excellence in Education, Research & Innovation

Khulna Khan Bahadur Ahsanullah University (KKBAU) was established by the Dhaka Ahsania Mission (DAM) in 2018. Dhaka Ahsania Mission is a renowned non-profit voluntary organization founded in 1958 by Khan Bahadur Ahsanullah, a distinguished educationist and social reformer of undivided India.

Inspired by his vision of a just, enlightened, and value-based society, KKBAU is committed to providing quality higher education, fostering research, innovation, and developing skilled graduates for national and global leadership.

Established under the permission of the Ministry of Education and the University Grants Commission (UGC) of Bangladesh, KKBAU received official approval in 2022 and began academic activities in January 2023.

Academic • 12 September 2026

KKBAU Celebrates EEE Day 2026 in a Festive Atmosphere; Calls for Alternative Energy Sources and Technological Innovation

Khulna Khan Bahadur Ahsanullah University (KKBAU) celebrated EEE Day 2026 on 12 September 2026 through a day-long program organized by the Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering (EEE) and the EEE Club. The event highlighted the importance of finding alternative sources of energy to reduce the country's dependence on oil and gas and encouraged students to engage in technological innovation and research. Speaking as the chief guest, Vice Chancellor (Designate) Professor Dr. Md. Anisur Rahman emphasized the growing importance of Electrical and Electronic Engineering in technology-driven development and industrial growth. He encouraged students to develop practical skills, innovative thinking, and research capabilities alongside their theoretical knowledge. The program featured a freshers' reception, technical workshop, project showcase, poster presentation, circuit-solving competition, and cultural program. Fifteen projects were presented at the project showcase, while students actively participated in various competitions and technical sessions. The day also marked the first anniversary of the EEE Club, which officially began its journey at KKBAU on this day in 2025.

University News • 5 August 2026

KKBAU Observes July Mass Uprising Day; Vice Chancellor Designate Calls the Spirit of July a Driving Force for Building an Inclusive Nation

Khulna Khan Bahadur Ahsanullah University (KKBAU) observed July Mass Uprising Day on 5 August 2026 with a discussion session held at the university conference hall. The program was attended by faculty members, students, officers, and staff. Vice Chancellor (Designate) Professor Dr. Md. Anisur Rahman, the chief guest, described the July Mass Uprising as a landmark chapter in Bangladesh's struggle for democracy, justice, and the protection of people's rights. He emphasized the importance of building an inclusive and discrimination-free society and urged the younger generation to uphold patriotism, ethics, responsibility, and humanitarian values. The event concluded with a special prayer and the screening of a documentary on the July 2024 Mass Uprising produced by the Department of Computer Science and Engineering (CSE), KKBAU.
