Welcome to Eastern University – A Leading Private University in Bangladesh

A premier institution dedicated to academic excellence and innovation in higher education

1

Establishment

Eastern University (EU) was established in 2003 under the Private University Act 1992 with the vision of creating a center of excellence in higher education in Bangladesh. Founded by a group of eminent academics, professionals, and philanthropists, the university was built on the values of quality education, ethical practices, and social responsibility.

2

Mission

From its inception, Eastern University set out to provide affordable world-class education, ensuring access for students from diverse backgrounds. Over the years, it has expanded its academic programs, offering undergraduate and postgraduate degrees in Business Administration, Law, Arts, Social Sciences, Computer Science, and Engineering.

3

Infrastructure

EU has continuously developed its academic and physical infrastructure, with modern classrooms, advanced laboratories, a well-equipped library, and digital learning resources. It emphasizes research, innovation, and industry collaboration, preparing graduates to meet the demands of a competitive global market.

4

Achievements

Today, Eastern University stands as one of the fastest-growing private universities in Bangladesh, known for its commitment to excellence, student-centered learning, and contribution to nation-building through quality education and research initiatives.
