Department of Theatre
Theatre Arts is a discipline that enables students to explore a range of debates in the Humanities as they inform - and are informed by - the theatre and performing arts landscape and its impact on contemporary life.
Course Details
Department of Music
The courses are designed to develop students creatively by imparting sound knowledge about music. Music is considered here in the larger background and context of society and culture.
Course Details
Department of Dance
Dance is now a subject of study at a higher level along with pursuit and graceful practice. Courses in this department are designed to develop students creatively by deepening their knowledge of dance.
Course Details
Department of Fashion Design
Fashion Design program’s of Tagore University of Creative Arts reputation is based on quality, relevance, and innovation. The cornerstone of the department’s curriculum is providing the students the opportunity to learn by working with successful industry professionals.
Course Details
Department of Business Administration
The Department of Business Administration offers an Undergraduate degree program in business education with a clear objective to develop competent and efficient business executives as well as entrepreneurs.

Let's explore your creativity

Tagore University of Creative Arts (TUCA) is a UGC approved private university of Bangladesh whose curriculum is based on the philosophy of the Nobel Laureate Rabindranath Tagore. The University was established in 2016 by the National Professor Dr. Anisuzzaman who served as the founding president of the board of trustees.
