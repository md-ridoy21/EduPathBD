About Our University

International Islami University of Science and Technology Bangladesh (IIUSTB) stands as a beacon of knowledge (‘ilm) and moral guidance. It was established in 2023 as a new generation digital private University in Bangladesh having full commitment and dedication for standard, quality, sincerity and excellence. It upholds the values of compassion, justice, and respect for all creation. It encourages the pursuit of wisdom through reason and reflection, as enjoined in the holy Qur'an. It is a place for the exploration of ideas and the sincere quest for truth.
