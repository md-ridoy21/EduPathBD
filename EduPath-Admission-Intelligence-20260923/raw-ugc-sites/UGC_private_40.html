Welcome to University of South Asia

University of South Asia is the University for Generation Bangladesh who aspires for an inclusive Bangladesh. It's the only private university that connects all the dots and let your network that you build here take care of your career.

For its outstanding central location in Amin Bazar, Savar, Dhaka, the University attracts students and staff from various backgrounds through its reputation and commitment to innovation and excellence in all activities. The campus offers an idyllic environment for an academic institution.
