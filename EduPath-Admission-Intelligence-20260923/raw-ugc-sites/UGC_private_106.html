A Few Words
About Our University

CBUFT is a prime professional educational institution in Bangladesh. Founded in 2022 by the Bangladesh Garments Manufactures and Exporters Association (BGMEA), CBUFT aims to develop skilled professionals with updated technical competence for apparel sector in Bangladesh. CBUFT is currently located in BGMEA Bhaban at South Khulshi, a prime location in the Chittagong Metropolitan City.

CBUFT at a glance

Chattogram BGMEA University of Fashion & Technology was founded with a goal to establish itself as a "Center-of-Excellence" in higher education and to produce graduates with interfacial skills, competencies and English language skills in order to meet the greater demand of textile and allied sectors of home and abroad emphasizing on innovation and creativity. Since its establishment, we haven't strayed from that vision.

An environment that helps develop your ability

Well-equipped facilities with such high quality that even professionals appreciate them

Chattogram BGMEA University of Fashion & Technology (CBUFT) is known for its state-of-the-art facilities that provide students with hands-on experience and prepare them for successful careers in the global fashion industry. The facilities are not only for students but also for professionals and researchers to access for professional training and research purposes. The facilities are of high quality and even professionals appreciate them. The University's facilities are designed to provide students with a comprehensive education and to help them stay ahead of the latest trends and technologies in the fashion industry.

Courses in specialist fields are designed to allow students to become what they want to be

Chattogram BGMEA University of Fashion & Technology (CBUFT) offers a range of courses that are designed to allow students to become experts in their field of interest. The university provides a wide range of courses in fashion design, textile engineering, merchandising and management. With a focus on hands-on learning and industry-relevant curriculum, CBUFT provides students with the skills and knowledge they need to succeed in their chosen field. The university also offers specialist courses such as sustainable fashion and textile engineering, which enables students to gain expertise in specific areas of the fashion industry.

Fully supporting the learning of all levels of students under a tutoring system

Chattogram BGMEA University of Fashion & Technology (CBUFT) is known for its fully supportive tutoring system for all levels of students. The university has a dedicated team of experienced faculty and staff who provide personalized guidance and mentorship to students. With small class sizes and hands-on learning opportunities, CBUFT ensures that each student receives the individual attention they need to succeed. This approach to education has resulted in a high success rate for CBUFT graduates, who are well-prepared for successful careers in the fashion industry.

Helping you settle into life in Bangladesh

Chattogram BGMEA University of Fashion & Technology (CBUFT) is not only dedicated to providing a splendor education in fashion and technology but also to helping students settle into life in Bangladesh. The university offers a range of support services, including accommodation, language classes and cultural orientation programs to assist students in adjusting to the local culture and way of life. CBUFT provides a warm and inclusive environment that helps students feel at home, allowing them to fully immerse themselves in their studies and enjoy their time in Bangladesh.
