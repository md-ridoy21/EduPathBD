20 Jun 2026

Reporting on Defense, Intelligence & Military Beat

The Faculty of Social Science & Liberal Arts Club at UCSI University Bangladesh Branch Campus hosted an insightful session featuring Abu Rushd A R M Shahidul Islam, Editor in Chief of the Bangladesh Defence Journal. The lecture focused on the ethics, responsibilities, and challenges of reporting on defense, intelligence, and military affairs while balancing public interest and national security. Students gained valuable knowledge from an experienced media professional on the importance of responsible and ethical journalism.
