About the University

University of Asia PacificExcellence in Higher Education Since 1996

University of Asia Pacific (UAP) was established in 1996 with a vision to enhance the opportunity for higher education in Bangladesh. The University, under the Private University Act 1992, started its operation by offering four-year Bachelor Degree Programs in Computer Science & Technology and Business Administration.

Today, UAP offers undergraduate programs in multiple disciplines including Engineering, Business, Law, Pharmacy, and Architecture. All curricula are approved by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and the Government of Bangladesh.

  • UGC & Government approved curricula
  • Washington Accord accredited degrees
  • QS Asia ranked #681-700
  • Vibrant research & innovation culture
