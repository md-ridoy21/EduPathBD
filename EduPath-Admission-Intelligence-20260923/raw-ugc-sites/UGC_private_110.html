What We Offer

At Lalon University of Science & Arts, Kushtia, students receive a quality education guided by expert faculty, supported by modern facilities and innovative learning tools.

Experienced Faculty

Our dedicated and highly qualified faculty members bring academic expertise and real-world insights to help students excel.

Advanced I-EMS System

Our I-EMS system allows students to access their portal, pay fees online, check results, and stay connected with campus services.

Modern Library

Our well-stocked library offers a rich collection of books, journals, and digital resources to support academic and research needs.

Modern Laboratories

State-of-the-art labs for science, engineering, and technology programs ensure hands-on learning and skill development.

Academic Programs

LUSA offer programs in Law, Arts, Business, and Technology, including Law, English, Public Administration, Banking & Finance, CSE, and EEE.

Digital Learning Support

Blended learning combines classroom teaching with online resources, making education more flexible and interactive.

CSE

B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)

CSE covers programming, algorithms, database systems, artificial intelligence, and software development. Students work on practical projects to solve real-world problems using technology. Graduates are well-prepared for roles in software engineering, IT consultancy, and emerging tech industries.

Apply now

EEE

B.Sc. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering (EEE)

EEE offers in-depth knowledge of electrical systems, electronics, power engineering, and communication technology. Students engage in hands-on lab work and projects to strengthen technical skills. Career opportunities include energy, telecommunications, automation, and electronics manufacturing.

Apply now

English

B.A. (Hons) in English

The English program develops communication skills, literary appreciation, and analytical thinking. Students explore literature, linguistics, and cultural studies, enhancing their ability to express ideas effectively in both academic and professional settings. This degree opens opportunities in education, media, publishing, and global communication.

Apply now

Public Administration

BSS (Hons) in Public Administration

Public Administration focuses on governance, policy-making, and management in public service. Students learn how to design, implement, and evaluate public policies while developing leadership and problem-solving abilities. Graduates can work in government agencies, NGOs, and international organizations.

Apply now

BBA

BBA (Hons) in Banking & Finance

This program provides knowledge in financial systems, banking operations, investment strategies, and risk management. Students gain analytical skills to make informed financial decisions and understand market dynamics. Career paths include banking, corporate finance, insurance, and investment firms.

Apply now

LAW

Bachelor of Laws

Department of Law is approved by UGC and admission will commence once the NOC is obtained from Bangladesh Bar Council.

Apply now
