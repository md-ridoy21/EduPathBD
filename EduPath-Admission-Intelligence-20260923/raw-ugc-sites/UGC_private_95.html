About Us

As a distinguished sister concern of Ahsanullah University of Science and Technology, our university is dedicated to providing world-class education in Bangladesh. Sponsored by the Dhaka Ahsania Mission, a respected organization committed to social development, we offer a dynamic learning environment that prepares students for meaningful careers and impactful contributions to society. Our programs are fully approved by the University Grants Commission (UGC), ensuring that our curriculum meets high standards of quality and relevance. 

We offer a range of undergraduate and postgraduate programs, including Bachelor’s degrees in English, Computer Science and Engineering (CSE), Electrical and Electronic Engineering (EEE), Civil Engineering (CE), and Business Administration (BBA), along with an Executive MBA (EMBA) for working professionals. Our diverse offerings are designed to cultivate critical thinking, technical skills, and leadership qualities, equipping students to succeed in a rapidly changing world.

As part of the Ahsanullah academic family, we embrace a tradition of promoting science, technology, and social progress. Our mission is to foster a generation of forward-thinking individuals who are well-prepared to drive positive change in Bangladesh and beyond.
