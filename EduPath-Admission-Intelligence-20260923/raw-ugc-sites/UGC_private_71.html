Welcome to NBIU

North Bengal International University (Bengali: নর্থ বেঙ্গল ইন্টারন্যাশনাল ইউনিভার্সিটি), a beacon of educational excellence was established on 15 September, 2013 with the approval and endorsement of the Government of the Peoples Republic of Bangladesh and UGC (University Grants Commission). Our university, guided by a vision of academic prowess and social advancement, is proud to host a diverse community of students, faculty, and staff from around the globe.

Vision : Our vision is to emerge as a premier educational institution in the country, offering high-quality education accessible to all, fostering the growth of future generations. At NBIU, we are committed to providing a nurturing environment where students can flourish academically, intellectually, and personally. Our aim is to empower students with the knowledge, skills, and values necessary to thrive in a rapidly changing world.

Mission : NBIU is dedicated to advancing higher education and facilitating sustainable economic development by empowering proficient graduates in various disciplines. To enact our vision, we are committed to cultivating the following qualities in our students and in our institution:
            1) Student centered pedagogy is our primary goal.
            2) Critical thinkers with the ability to generate insights and necessary analytical skills.
            3) Efficient in written, oral and digital communication.
            4) Lifelong learners with excellent leadership skills and self-inspired innovators.
            5) Citizens conscious of social justice and responsive towards sustainability.

Join us as we embark on a journey of discovery, innovation, and collaboration. Whether you're a prospective student, a dedicated faculty member, or a passionate researcher, we welcome you to be a part of our vibrant academic community at North Bengal International University.
