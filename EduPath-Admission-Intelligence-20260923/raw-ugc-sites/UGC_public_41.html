    About University

    University of Frontier Technology, Bangladesh (UFTB), is a specialized university in Bangladesh. The Government of the People's Republic of Bangladesh enacted an act in Parliament on 26 July 2016, to establish this university. The university is in Kaliakair upazila, in Gazipur District.  In line with the Fourth Industrial Revolution, the university aims to create opportunities for higher education and research in Bangladesh, keeping pace with the advanced world in the fields of science, engineering, and technology. It emphasizes modern knowledge, especially in information and technology, conducts teaching and research activities, and focuses on the development of new technologies.
