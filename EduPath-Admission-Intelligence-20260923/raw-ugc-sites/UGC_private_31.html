2026 5th International Conference on Innovation and Transformation for Development

The 5th International Conference on Innovation and Transformation for Development (ITD-2026) is an international initiative of Green University of Bangladesh (GUB) to expedite the spirit of innovation and transformation for development through promoting intellectual discourse and dialogues among academics, scholars, professionals and industry leaders around the globe in the fields of Arts and Social Sciences to be held on 27 and 28 November, 2025 onsite in our Permanent Campus at Purbachal American City, Kanchan, Rupganj, Narayanganj-1461, Dhaka, Bangladesh.

Visit Us

2026 8th International conference on Sustainable Technologies for Industry 5.0

STI 2026 welcomes researchers, industry professionals, and practitioners to display their scientific achievements and sustainable solutions for industrial developments in the fields of computer science & engineering, electrical & electronic engineering, textile engineering, robotics and cyber-physical systems related to Industry 5.0

Visit Us

Planet LangLit 2026: International Conference on Language, Literature and Cultural Studies

International Conference on Language, Literature and Cultural Studies is a landmark academic initiative of Green University of Bangladesh (GUB), organized in partnership with leading universities and research institutions from Bangladesh, India, Egypt, Spain, and the United States. This pioneering event is designed to advance critical conversations and collaborative research at the intersection of language, literature, education, and ecology, with a central focus on planetary justice and inclusive futures.
