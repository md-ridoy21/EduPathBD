Message From

Vice Chancellor

It is my great pleasure and privilege to welcome you to the official website of Patuakhali Science and Technology University (PSTU). Established in 2002 as the first public university in the Barishal Division of Bangladesh, PSTU has grown steadily into a prominent center of excellence in higher education, research, innovation, and community engagement. Over the years, the University has earned the trust and confidence of people at home and abroad through its commitment to quality education, impactful research, human capital development, and service to society.
