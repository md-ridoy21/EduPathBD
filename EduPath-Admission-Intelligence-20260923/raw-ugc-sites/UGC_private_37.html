About the University

Metropolitan University, Sylhet, Bangladesh is a leading private university committed to quality education, innovative research, and holistic student development. The university offers a supportive academic environment that equips students with the skills and values needed to succeed in a competitive global society.

Our Academic Schools

School of Science & Technology

Read More

The School of Science & Technology is dedicated to advancing knowledge, innovation, and research across science, technology, and computing. It focuses on developing computational methods, data analysis techniques, and technology-driven solutions while providing modern computing resources and an environment that supports teaching, learning, and research.

School of Business & Economics

Read More

The School of Business & Economics is committed to developing future business leaders, entrepreneurs, and professionals through excellence in education, research, and innovation. It provides students with a strong foundation in business, economics, management, finance, and related disciplines, preparing them to make informed decisions and contribute meaningfully to a dynamic and evolving global economy.

Academic Departments
Business Administration Economics

School of Law

Read More

The School of Law is dedicated to excellence in legal education, research, and professional development. It equips students with a strong understanding of legal principles, justice, governance, and ethical practice while developing the analytical, critical-thinking, and advocacy skills needed to navigate an evolving legal landscape and contribute meaningfully to society.
