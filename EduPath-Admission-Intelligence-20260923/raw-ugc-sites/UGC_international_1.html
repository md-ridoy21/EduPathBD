IUT Mars Rover - European Rover Challenge 
Team ​Avijatrik​ advances to the European Rover Challenge (ERC) 2022 Onsite final round, qualifying in the preliminary round as one of the top 11 out of selected 16 teams.
The final round of ERC On-site edition 2022 will take place from September 9 to September 11, 2022, on the campus of the Kielce University of Technology in Poland.

IUT Mars Rover participated in the Remote version of the European Rover Challenge (ERC) competition.
