About Us

Brahmaputra International University was established in Jamalpur district and granted approval by the University Grants Commission and Ministry of Education in 2013 as a result of the tireless efforts of renowned education evangelist Shahidur Rahman Khan (Retired Principal Govt. Ashek Mahmud College, Jamalpur). In the Mymensingh division, it is the regions first private university. At Foujdari Moor, regular classes are being conducted for the students at the permanent campus on the vast land beside the historical river, Brahmaputra river (Centre point of Jamalpur town). English is the language of instruction and examination at BRIU. Nine departments, including CSE, EEE, Business Administration (BBA, EMBA), English, Economics, Political Science.
