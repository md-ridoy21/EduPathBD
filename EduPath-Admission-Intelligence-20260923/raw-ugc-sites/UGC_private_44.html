About US

Established in 2003 as Bangladesh's first IT-based private university, UITS is dedicated to providing high-quality technical education and producing skilled professionals.

Administration

The university is governed by a Board of Trustees, chaired by the founder of PHP Family, Al-haj Sufi Mohammed Mizanur Rahman, who ensures the strategic vision of the institution.

Departments

UITS offers a wide range of undergraduate and graduate programs under the faculties of Science and Engineering, Business, Liberal Arts, and Law.

Research

UITS promotes innovation through its dedicated Research Center, focusing on diverse fields such as Machine Learning, Cyber Security, Civil Litigation, and sustainable business practices.

UITS

University of Information Technology and Sciences (UITS), the first IT-based private university in Bangladesh was founded on 7 August 2003 as a non-profit organization & EIIN is 136666. University of Information Technology & Sciences (UITS), a concern of PHP Family headed by Alhaj Sufi Mohamed Mizanur Rahman is the sponsor of UITS.
