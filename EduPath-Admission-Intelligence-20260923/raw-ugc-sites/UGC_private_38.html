About Uttara University

Shaping Leaders Since 2003

Established in 2003, Uttara University has been at the forefront of higher education in Bangladesh. Located in the heart of Dhaka, our institution is committed to providing world-class education, fostering innovation, and preparing students for global challenges.

With modern facilities, distinguished faculty members, and a vibrant campus life, we offer an unparalleled learning experience.
