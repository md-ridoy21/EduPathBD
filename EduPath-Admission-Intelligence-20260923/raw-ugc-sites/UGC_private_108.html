A FEW WORDS ABOUT TEESTA UNIVERSITY

Teesta University is the first private university of Rangpur, located at Modern More, R. K. Road, Rangpur, designed to deliver student-centered teaching, research orientation, and service to society.

Teesta University is established under the auspices of the Teesta University Trust and sanctioned by the Ministry of Education in accordance with the Private University Act of 2010.

“

Teesta University is named after the Teesta River, which flows through Bangladesh and India and is a vital source of water and livelihood for millions of people. The river also symbolizes the potential for cooperation and development in the region, as well as the challenges of environmental conservation and social justice. Our university aims to reflect these values and aspirations in its academic programs, research activities, and community engagement.

We are grateful for the support and guidance of the government, the University Grants Commission, and other stakeholders who have helped us realize our dream of establishing Teesta University. We are also proud of our partnerships with national and international organizations, institutions, and experts who have contributed to our academic development and quality assurance. We look forward to expanding our network of collaboration and exchange in the future.

The University is committed to advancing knowledge transfer, innovating new knowledge, building skilled manpower, and eliminating regional educational disparities. As we look ahead to the future, I want to assure you that the Board of Trustees will always be devoted to supporting the University’s mission and achieving its educational goals.

Md. Shahzada Al Sadik Registrar
“

I am deeply humbled and honoured to have been chosen by the distinguished President of the People’s Republic of Bangladesh, His Excellency the Chancellor, as the Treasurer of Teesta University, Rangpur. My sincere appreciation extends to the reverend Board of Trustees, with special gratitude to our esteemed Chairman, Mr. Asraful Alam Al-Amin.

Having devoted thirty-seven (37) years to serving the nation in various educational roles, I am privileged to have received a national accolade—a gold medal from the Government of Bangladesh for my contributions as an educator.

A university is not merely a seat of learning; it is a dynamic centre for the cultivation, dissemination, and dispersion of knowledge. With great optimism, I believe that Teesta University, in its youth, will emerge as a symbol of success in fulfilling the cherished aspirations of Bangladesh. At the forefront of our mission is the commitment to generate novel ideas and disseminate the latest advancements in basic research, particularly in cutting-edge technology.

Aligned with the forward-thinking initiatives of our present government, the Teesta University authority, in collaboration with our esteemed faculty, stands resolute in meeting the evolving needs of our time. As a united team, we are dedicated to contributing significantly to the realization of our collective vision as designed by The University Grants Commission (UGC) in its Outcome Based Education (OBE) curriculum to transform Bangladesh as a developed nation in near future.

Prof. Sonjib Chawdhury Treasurer
“

It is with immense pleasure and a deep sense of responsibility that I extend a warm welcome to you at Teesta University (TU), the first private university in the Rangpur District. Our journey begins in 2023 with a clear vision – to provide quality higher education that not only meets the needs of our nation but also aligns with the demands of the global landscape. As the Vice-Chancellor of TU, my mission is to create an academic environment that is safe, secure, and conducive to holistic growth. I am committed to developing TU into a center of excellence, fostering knowledge creation, and contributing to socio-economic and rural social development. To achieve this, we are embracing Outcome Based Education (OBE), conducting research studies tailored to current needs, and engaging in outreach activities to address the requirements of our community. Starting in January 2024, we are proud to offer academic programs in seven disciplines, namely Computer Science and Engineering, Electrical and Electronic Engineering, Information and Communication Engineering, Biochemistry and Molecular Biology, Agriculture, Veterinary Science and Animal Husbandry, and Bachelor of Business Administration. In addition to academic pursuits, TU will actively involve students in co-curricular and extra-curricular activities, ensuring a well-rounded educational experience. Key Initiatives to strengthen the university activities would be to establish: 1. Institutional Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC): Ensuring and enhancing the quality of education through ongoing monitoring and evaluation. 2. Research and Advisory Cell (RAC): Cultivating a culture of research and providing valuable advisory services. 3. Farmers’ Service Center (FSC): Bridging the gap between academia and agriculture by offering services to our farming community. 4. Mobile Veterinary and Agricultural Clinic (MoVAC): Taking education and healthcare directly to rural areas, ensuring the well-being of both animals and our agricultural community. 5. Entrepreneurship and Career Development Cell (ECDC): Nurturing an entrepreneurial spirit and providing guidance for career development. I invite each one of you to join us in this exciting journey of learning, innovation, and personal growth. Together, let us build a brighter future for our students and contribute meaningfully to society.

Professor Dr. M. Abul Kashem Vice Chancellor
