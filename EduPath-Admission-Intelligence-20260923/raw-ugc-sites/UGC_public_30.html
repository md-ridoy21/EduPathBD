Professor Dr. Md. Showkat Ali

Vice Chancellor, BRUR

Welcome to the official website of Begum Rokeya University, Rangpur (BRUR). It is an honor for me to serve as the Vice Chancellor of BRUR and I do hope your visit to this website will be meaningful and worthy of your time spent. I also would like to invite all bright academics and students from all over the globe to join us in this exciting experience of learning and research.

At BRUR, we believe that research is at the heart of academic discovery. Our faculty, colleagues, and students engage in cutting-edge research that addresses real-world challenges, driving progress and contributing to society. Through interdisciplinary collaboration, we aim to produce knowledge that not only advances our fields but also enhances the well-being of our communities.

Begum Rokeya University, Rangpur has played a significant role not only as a leader in higher education but also in nation-building since its establishment in 2008. Hence, during the recent July uprising of 2024, the university community played a critical role in shaping the national aspiration.

The July uprising has created a new set of expectations, opening a new window of opportunity for the university community to turn the nation’s aspirations into reality. In this context, we must embrace the new challenges ahead.

I invite you to explore our website and learn more about our programs, research initiatives, and community activities. Together, let us continue to build a bright future grounded in excellence and opportunity.

Thank you for being part of our journey.

Warm regards,
Professor Dr. Md. Showkat Ali
Vice Chancellor
Begum Rokeya University, Rangpur.
