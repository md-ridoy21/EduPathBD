OUR FACULTIES

Know About Our Faculties

World school of business

The World School of Business (WSB) is a premier institution offering undergraduate and graduate programs in management and entrepreneurship. With a focus on experiential learning, WSB prepares students for successful careers in the dynamic global business landscape.

Apply Now
Faculty of arts and humanities

The Faculty of Arts and Humanities is an academic division offering a range of undergraduate and graduate programs in the fields of arts, humanities, and social sciences. The faculty is known for its exceptional faculty members and commitment to research and intellectual pursuits.

Apply Now
Faculty of science

The Faculty of Science offers a diverse range of programs in science, technology and mathematics. With state-of-the-art facilities and expert faculty, it prepares students for careers in cutting-edge industries and in fields that drive innovation and discovery.

Apply Now
Faculty of engineering

The Faculty of Engineering offers a diverse range of programs in engineering and related technological fields. With state-of-the-art facilities and expert faculty, it prepares students for careers in cutting-edge industries that drive innovation and technological advancement.
