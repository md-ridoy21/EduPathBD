WELCOME TO TIMES UNIVERSITY BANGLADESH (TUB)

Welcome to our Times University Bangladesh- UGC & Government Approved Private University– committed to contribute in national development through producing efficient human resources. In south Bengal huge intending higher education seeking students run for Dhaka to study in the private university which makes them higher cost for acquiring higher education not even in a general quality standard. In view with a great thinking Times University has been established to provide higher education with quality by lower cost for the aspiring student of south Bengal including greater Faridpur, Barisal division and Khulna Division. We are striving to make your dream success.
