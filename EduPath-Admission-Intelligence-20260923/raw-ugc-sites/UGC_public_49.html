Welcome Message from the Vice-Chancellor

Agriculture remains key to food security, environmental sustainability, and socio-economic development. As one of the premier institutions of higher education in Bangladesh, Habiganj Agricultural University is committed to advancing knowledge through excellence in education, research, innovation, and community engagement. The university was indeed established with the aim of promoting agricultural education, research, and innovation, making a significant contribution to the advancement of sustainable agricultural practices and rural development. Our mission is to produce competent graduates, generate impactful research, and provide practical solutions to the evolving challenges of agriculture and rural development.

In an increasingly interconnected and technology-driven world, agricultural education must transcend traditional boundaries. We aspire to promote graduates who are intellectually curious, ethically responsible, and equipped with the knowledge, skills, and leadership needed to address national priorities while contributing to global sustainable development. Through interdisciplinary collaboration, scientific inquiry, digital innovation, and strong partnerships with farmers, industries, research organisations, and international institutions, we seek to transform knowledge into meaningful societal impact.

Our greatest strength lies in the dedication of our faculty, officers, staff, students, and alumni. We are developing an inclusive academic environment that champions creativity, critical thinking, lifelong learning, and academic excellence. By fostering a culture of integrity, teamwork, and innovation, we aim to establish Habiganj Agricultural University as a distinguished centre of excellence in agricultural education and research.

It is my pleasure to invite all members of our university community and our valued stakeholders to join us in this shared journey. Together, let us shape a future where knowledge drives innovation, research inspires progress, and education empowers sustainable development for Bangladesh and beyond.

Welcome Message from the Pro Vice-Chancellor

Warm Greetings and a Hearty Welcome to Habiganj Agricultural University

We are incredibly honored to have you join our active academic community. My primary goal as the Pro-Vice-Chancellor is to create a vibrant, welcoming, and intellectually stimulating atmosphere where quality is pursued and curiosity flourishes.

With its cutting-edge facilities, good teachers, and research opportunities, Habiganj Agricultural University is steadfastly dedicated to providing top-notch education. We seek to develop critical thinkers, creative thinkers, and caring global citizens who can steer the world of tomorrow rather than merely imparting knowledge.

We go above and above to help your personal and professional development, whether you are starting out as a student or working with us as a researcher or faculty member. I urge you to take advantage of the many possibilities, resources, and community.

I hope the journey ahead of you is transforming, fulfilling, and inspirational.

ওয়েবসাইটটি বর্তমানে রক্ষণাবেক্ষণের অধীনে রয়েছে। সাময়িক অসুবিধার জন্য আন্তরিকভাবে দুঃখিত। আপনার ধৈর্য এবং সহযোগিতার জন্য ধন্যবাদ। ১৮ জুন হবিগঞ্জ কৃষি বিশ্ববিদ্যালয়ে স্নাতক (লেভেল-১, সেমি-১), ২য় ধাপের ভর্তি বিজ্ঞপ্তি (২০২৪-২৫)। ২০২৪-২৫ শিক্ষাবর্ষে স্নাতক (লেভেল-১, সেমিস্টার-১) শ্রেণীতে হবিগঞ্জ কৃষি বিশ্ববিদ্যালয়ে সুযোগ পাওয়া ছাত্র-ছাত্রীদের (১ম,২য় এবং ৩য় ধাপে অনলাইনে ভর্তিকৃত ) সশরীরে উপস্থিত হয়ে আগামী ১৬-০৭-২০২৫ তারিখে ভর্তি সংক্রান্ত বিজ্ঞপ্তি। ২০২৪-২০২৫ শিক্ষাবর্ষের নবাগত শিক্ষার্থীদের ওরিয়েন্টেশন উপলক্ষ্যে বিজ্ঞপ্তি (09 December 2025) শরীফ ওসমান হাদীর মৃত্যুতে হবিগঞ্জ কৃষি বিশ্ববিদ্যালয়ে শোক বার্তা প্রকাশ মেধাভিত্তিক ও বৈষম্যমুক্ত বিশ্ববিদ্যালয় গড়ার অঙ্গীকার নিয়ে যোগদান করেছেন হকৃবির নতুন উপাচার্য অধ্যাপক ড. সৈয়দ সায়েম উদ্দিন আহম্মদ
