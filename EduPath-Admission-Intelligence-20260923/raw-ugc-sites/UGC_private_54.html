Hamdard University Bangladesh has recently received approval from the University Grants Commission (UGC) for its new program, Bachelor of Arts (Honours) in Islamic Studies. || Admission going on Fall 2026 : BBA, BSc in CSE (Regular & Diploma), BSc in EEE (Regular & Diploma), BSc (Honours) in Mathematics, BA (Honours) in English, BSS (Honours) in Economics, BA (Honours) in Islamic Studies, MBA, EMBA, MA in ELT, MSc in Mathematics, MPH and MA in Islamic Studies (2-year).
