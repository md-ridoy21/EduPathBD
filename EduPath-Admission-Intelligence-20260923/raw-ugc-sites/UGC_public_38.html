Message the Vice Chancellor

It is an immense honor to serve as the Vice-Chancellor of Chittagong Medical University (CMU), a prestigious institution dedicated to advancing medical education, research, and healthcare services in Bangladesh. Established through Act No. 17 of the National Parliament on May 12, 2016, CMU is committed to excellence in higher education, medical innovation, and patient care.

As we embark on this journey, our goal is to expand academic opportunities, foster groundbreaking research, and enhance medical facilities to ensure that our graduates are well-equipped to meet the evolving challenges of the healthcare sector. Through collaboration, dedication, and innovation, we aim to strengthen the university’s role in shaping the future of medical sciences in Bangladesh and beyond.

Prof. Dr. Omar Faruque Yusuf
