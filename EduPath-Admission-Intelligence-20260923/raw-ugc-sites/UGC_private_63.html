Welcome to SU

Sonargaon
University

Sonargaon University (SU) is one of Bangladesh’s preeminent private universities, approved by the University Grants Commission (UGC) of Bangladesh and the Government of Bangladesh, and sponsored by the Sonargaon University (SU) Trust.

SU offers undergraduate and graduate programs through the Faculty of Science & Engineering, Faculty of Business and Faculty of Arts & Humanities—combining modern curricula, well-equipped laboratories, experienced faculty and strong industry linkage.
