The People’s University Of Bangladesh

The People’s University of Bangladesh (PUB) was established on 14th May, 1996 as a private university under the Private University Act 1992, with a vision to enhance the opportunities for higher education in Bangladesh. The University Grants Commission (UGC) of Bangladesh has approved its curriculum.
