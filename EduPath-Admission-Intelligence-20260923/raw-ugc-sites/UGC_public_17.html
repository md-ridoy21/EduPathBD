Professor Dr. Md. Abdul Latif

Vice Chancellor

Message From Vice Chancellor

It is a great pleasure for me to be the Vice-Chancellor of Sher-e-Bangla Agricultural University (SAU), Dhaka. The bucolic serene green campus is located in the heart of the gleeful Dhaka city. Over the last 77 years, it has transformed into a public university with three faculties and produced more than 14000 agricultural graduates those have founded the firm base of agriculture of the nation.

The mission of Sher-e-Bangla Agricultural University is based upon the firm belief that agricultural education, particularly higher education provides a critical pathway to leadership development in academic and research fields as well as in achieving sustainable agricultural development, socio-economic progress of the country.

Presently, SAU is offering undergraduate and postgraduate programs through a course credit system with the aim to producing researchers, academicians and field level extension specialists in the field of Agriculture to deal with the issues like food production, poverty and hunger alleviation, food security, Protein Energy Malnutrition (PEM), Micro-Nutrient Deficiency (MND) etc. About 322 highly qualified faculty members are shouldering the academic responsibility as well as other research activities of the university.

Message From Treasurer

It is a matter of great honor and pride for me to serve as the Treasurer of Sher-e-Bangla Agricultural University, a historic institution dedicated to advancing agricultural education and research in Bangladesh. Since its establishment, SAU has played a vital role in producing skilled graduates and contributing to national food security, sustainable agriculture, and rural development.

As Treasurer, my priority is to ensure financial transparency, efficiency, and accountability in all administrative and developmental activities of the university. I am committed to utilizing resources effectively to strengthen academic programs, research facilities, and student support services. Together with our faculty, staff, and students, we aim to uphold SAU’s mission of innovation, excellence, and service to the nation. I firmly believe that through prudent financial management and strategic planning, we can continue to expand opportunities for learning, research, and community engagement that will benefit not only our university but also the wider society.

Sher-e-Bangla Agricultural University

Sher-e-Bangla Agricultural University (SAU) is located in the heart of the capital city, Dhaka with excellent public transportation facilities to reach the university. The SAU campus stands on 86.92 acres (35.19 ha) of picturesque land covered by green plantations.

Faculty and Department

Sher-e-Bangla Agricultural University (SAU), the oldest agricultural institution in Bangladesh, is home to several dedicated faculties that provide high-quality education and research opportunities in various areas of agriculture and life sciences. The university currently has four main faculties: the Faculty of Agriculture, Faculty of Agribusiness Management, Faculty of Animal Science & Veterinary Medicine, and Faculty of Fisheries & Aquaculture.

Each faculty is committed to producing skilled graduates, advancing scientific research, and addressing real-world challenges in agriculture, food security, animal health, and sustainable farming. Through modern laboratories, experienced faculty members, and practical field-based learning, SAU continues to play a vital role in shaping the future of Bangladesh’s agricultural development.
