Welcome To Fareast International University (FIU)

Fareast International University (FIU) is a fast growing private university in Bangladesh founded in 2013 by a group of philanthropists, intellectuals and industrialists. Its establishment got approval from the government in 2013 under the Private University Act-2010. The university started its  academic activities formally from Spring-2014 semester.

The primary aim of FIU is to generate knowledge by providing high quality education with academic excellence to produce skillful manpower and human resources who will accept the challenges of global corporate world. One of its objectives is to provide quality education at an affordable cost.

FIU is operating trimester basis in 18 Undergraduate & Master programs under 5 faculty, namely Faculty of Business, Faculty of Engineering, Faculty of Science, Faculty of Liberal Arts & Social Science and Faculty of Law.

The Administrative functionaries are experienced and accountable to the Board of Trustees and the management is discharging their responsibilities. All are unwaveringly committed to ensuring quality education in conducting academic environment and excellent infrastructure at the prime location at Uttara, Dhaka.

Message from the Vice Chancellor​

Established in 2013, Fareast International University is a modern, innovative institution dedicated to advancing higher education and driving Bangladesh’s national development. The university is committed to academic excellence, equipping students with the skills, curious mindset, and professional grounding needed to thrive in a rapidly changing world. In further advancing research and development, active collaboration is at the heart of the university. By partnering closely with industry, community, and global academic networks, the university prepares smart and entrepreneurial graduates ready to make a tangible impact in the workplace. As Vice Chancellor, I am privileged to lead FIU’s ambitious path forward. I invite you to join our growing academic community and help us make a lasting difference by serving mankind.
