Office Overview

The Admission and Registration Cell of the National University is one of the most important departments of the university, which is responsible for managing the admission and registration activities of students in all affiliated government and private colleges of the country. Through an integrated online management based on information technology, this department receives applications for admission to various educational programs, verifies applications, completes the admission process based on merit and qualifications, conducts registration activities and issues registration cards to admitted students.

The Admission and Registration Cell stores, manages and provides online services for all [Honours, Degree (Pass), Honours Professional, Preliminary to Masters, Masters Final, Masters Professional, PGD courses, M.Phil, PhD, On-Campus Honours and Masters programs] admission and registration related information through the National University's powerful, secure and modern central server-based information system. In addition to preparing and providing registration cards to admitted students online, the necessary information is made easily available to affiliated colleges. This integrated digital system ensures transparency, accuracy, data security and speed of service in the admission and registration process.

Since its inception, the admission and registration information of about 4 million students studying and completing their studies under the National University is securely stored in the central database of this department. Through maximum use of modern information technology, reliable information management and efficient services, the Admission and Registration Department plays an important role in making the academic activities of the National University more dynamic, transparent, effective and student-friendly.
